## Preview and GA status

Microsoft's Foundry status is feature-specific. Core agents are GA, while multiple capabilities such as memory, workflows, some tracing paths, monitoring, and some agent controls remain Preview or limited preview. Guidance should verify the exact feature rather than treating the whole platform as uniformly GA.
