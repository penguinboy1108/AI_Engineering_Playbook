## Promoted into existing canonical pages

### 1. Action-level authorization + behavioural boundary verification

**Evidence classification:** official Anthropic containment engineering guidance; official Microsoft Foundry preview implementation; cross-source engineering synthesis.

**Problem:** sandbox and egress allowlists constrain reachability, but do not prove a specific reachable action is authorized. Agent self-explanation is not an independent policy source.

**Change:** expand `agent-capability-containment.md` with canonical action record (resource, verb, authenticated identity, payload class), independent allow/deny/escalate enforcement, bounded executor, independent receipts, and positive/negative boundary probes. Test alternate paths and redirects. Keep Foundry hosted-agent network egress controls explicitly **Preview / not intended for production**, not a recommended production dependency.

**Sources:**
- https://www.anthropic.com/research/how-we-contain-claude
- https://www.anthropic.com/news/security
- https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/foundry/agents/how-to/add-hosted-agent-guardrails

### 2. Receipt reconciliation for at-least-once side effects

**Evidence classification:** official Microsoft runtime guarantee; engineering synthesis.

**Problem:** a durable activity can finish an external write, crash before recording its result, and be replayed. This is not exactly-once execution.

**Change:** extend `bounded-failure-evaluation.md` with stable operation IDs, authoritative external receipts, idempotency/lookup constraints, ambiguity escalation, and a crash-after-commit-before-ack regression fixture. Do not claim a local flag or unsupported idempotency key guarantees exactly-once business effects.

**Sources:**
- https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/durable-task/common/programming-model-overview
- https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/azure-functions/durable/durable-functions-external-events
